Ask a model to fill a schema field whose fact isn't in the document. If the schema forbids `null`, it has two options: break the contract, or invent the fact.

<PlateFigure slug="schemabench" />

## The trap split

- Nullable traps: honesty is free. Forced traps: honesty is impossible.
- Without nullable traps, "strict mode fabricates" is true by construction.

## Same facts, four shapes, three delivery modes

- Tiers T1–T4 generated from one field spec; a test asserts "same facts" for all 16 combinations.
- prompt_only / tool_call / strict; the schema is in the prompt in all three (state the limitation).

## Grading without a judge

- schema_valid, the 11-class violation taxonomy, field accuracy, fabrication, availability.
- Availability ≠ competence: 502s and length-truncated reasoning responses are excluded, not failed.

## Engineering under a 50-request/day cap

- Content-addressed cache as primary data; re-grade offline after any grader change.
- Mock-first, so the first real call was the experiment.
- The null-masking bug at T4 a failing test caught before any call was spent.

## What the probe found

- Mode support across 8 models (data/results/probe.json).
- One model rejected response_format outright (HTTP 400).
- Say plainly what's underpowered.
